## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored in the words, generic in the pixels. Two things are genuinely this product's: the Loader (CONCEVOIR/CODER/LANCER/CROITRE is the service sequence, not a spinner) and the French copy. Underneath, the visual layer is a neobrutalist kit-of-parts: every card is 4px border + 8px8px shadow + one of four accents, with no rule about WHEN each accent means what. Find-and-replace "developpeur" with "osteopathe" and the page survives untouched.

**Deterministic scan.** Static CLI scan clean (0 findings). Runtime in-page scan: 28 findings across 20 element groups. nested-cards x8 (Process + FAQ), line-length x4 (FAQ answers at ~93 chars/line, target <80), all-caps-body x5, tight-leading x3, oversized-h1 (80px for 129 chars), cramped-padding on hero yellow block (8px vertical for 56px text, needs 17).

**False positives dismissed:** 3 findings on span.sr-only (1x1px element), two body-level advisories, marquee overflow (+3072px is the intended clipped track).

**Where the passes disagree:** mechanical scan sampled 4 contrast pairs, all AA-compliant. Design review found one it had not sampled: white on #FF007F at 3.78:1, below AA. The review wins: the sample was incomplete, not the page compliant.

## Overall Impression

Emotional peak is at second 0. The loader is the best-designed moment; the curtain-to-hero handoff is genuinely well-made. Then decline: hero arrives collided, Problem/Expertise/Process is a flat plateau of equally-loud cards, Projects is the trough (one site and a question mark at the exact moment a prospect looks for proof). The end is a broken promise: the closing screen says "commence ici" and the button below does nothing. Peak-end rule: remembered as great intro, dead end. The exact opposite of the copy's thesis.

## What's Working

1. **The loader earns its cost.** Four words are the service sequence. Counter and bar driven from one tween so they cannot desync. Curtain onStart fires the hero entrance: one continuous move, not two animations stapled together.
2. **Motion is engineered.** prefers-reduced-motion honored at both entry points, everything inside gsap.context with revert, loops paused on visibilitychange, separate transform channels. Verified live: reduced motion loads a full, visible, static page.
3. **The French copy is the product.** Objection-first ordering, concrete numbers, FAQ reframes instead of reassures.
